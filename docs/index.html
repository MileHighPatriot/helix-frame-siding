How a job moves

Seven marks from the first walk to the punch.

  1. 01

    Consult

    We walk the site, measure what is actually there, and name the work Helix will self-perform.

  2. 02

    Estimate

    A written scope for framing, envelope, and any trade we will carry on our schedule.

  3. 03

    Permit

    We assemble the packet, or pull the permit, for the jurisdiction that owns the address.

  4. 04

    Frame

    Walls, floors, roofs, beams, and the temporary bracing that keeps the house true.

  5. 05

    Dry-in

    Sheathing, windows set with the right partners, and a roof that can take weather.

  6. 06

    Siding

    Rainscreen, flashings, and the finish skin: lap, panel, or board-and-batten.

  7. 07

    Punch

    A walk with the owner. Open items get a date, not a shrug.
