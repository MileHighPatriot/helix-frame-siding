Siding

A straight frame is only half the promise. Helix installs fiber-cement lap, panel, and board-and-batten over a drained cavity, with flashings that are drawn before they are bent.

Estimate this scope

What is in the scope

  • Weather barrier, furring, and rainscreen cavities
  • Fiber cement, wood, and engineered lap or panel
  • Window, door, and roof-to-wall flashing details
  • Soffit, fascia, and trim that lines up with the frame

Typical sequence

  1. 01Read the wall section and mark every penetration
  2. 02Install the drainage plane and flash the openings
  3. 03Hang the finish courses to a story pole, not to the eye
  4. 04Seal, caulk, and walk the elevations with the owner
