Berkeley deck and stair

A framed cedar deck and a switchback stair that replaced a rotting platform off a brick foursquare.

DeckingFraming
Deck
420 sf
Drop
4 ft

The constraint

The brick rim would not take a conventional ledger, and the yard dropped four feet to the garage.

Outcome

The deck is independent of the brick, and the stair lands at the garage without a makeshift step.

Scope

  1. 01Freestanding frame with a flashed ledger used only as a lateral tie
  2. 02Cedar joists, a picture-frame border, and hidden fasteners
  3. 03Switchback stair with a mid landing
  4. 04Cable-and-wood guard coordinated to the stair code
